# Source Map and Coverage

Create a source map before assigning batches. For each source, record `Source ID`, name, URL/file path, source class, geography, subcategory, date accessed, expected contribution, and limitations.

## Source classes

- Government, regulator, tax, or corporate registry
- Sector association or accreditation directory
- Official organization website or filing
- Funder, university, public agency, or institutional partner
- Reputable sector publication, ranking, or conference directory
- User-provided source or prior roster

## Prominence rubric

Assign a tier using evidence rather than intuition:

- `Tier 1 - Core`: repeatedly appears in authoritative sector sources, has clear national/regional significance, or is a central institution in the defined sector.
- `Tier 2 - Prominent`: strong regional, specialist, scale, funding, membership, or market significance with credible evidence.
- `Tier 3 - Relevant`: plausible in-scope organization included for coverage, but prominence is narrower or less well documented.
- `Candidate - Needs Review`: identity, scope, or prominence remains unresolved.

Record the rationale in one sentence and cite the source supporting it. Do not use search-result rank alone as a prominence signal.

## Coverage memo

Report counts by:

- prominence tier;
- organization type;
- subcategory;
- geography;
- discovery source class;
- included, excluded, deferred, and unresolved status.

Explain obvious gaps, source limitations, duplicate treatment, and whether the result is comprehensive, coverage-limited, or a starting roster.
